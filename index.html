📖 How to Use This App

1. Select Medium: Choose between Water/Glycol mix or Air.

2. Select System Type:
• Batch: For closed tanks, storage vessels, or room air volumes. Toggle volume units between litres (L) and cubic metres (m³).
• Flow Rate: For inline heaters, coils, boilers, or pump loops. Toggle flow units between L/s and m³/s.

3. Select What to Solve:
• Batch Mode: Calculate time required (seconds/hours) OR power required (kW). Target time can be entered in seconds or as minutes and seconds.
• Flow Mode: Calculate power required (kW) for a given flow OR temperature rise (ΔT) produced by a heater.

4. Display: Switch Day or Night mode from the top of the app.
