📖 How to Use This App

1. Select Medium: Choose between Water/Glycol mix or Air.

2. Select System Type:
• Batch: For closed tanks, storage vessels, or room air volumes. Toggle volume units between litres (L) and cubic metres (m³).
• Flow Rate: For inline heaters, coils, boilers, or pump loops. Toggle flow units between L/s, m³/h and m³/s.

3. Heating or Cooling: Sets whether the temperature rises or drops from the start temperature.

4. Select What to Solve:
• Batch Mode: Calculate time required OR power required (kW). Target time can be entered in seconds or as minutes and seconds.
• Flow Mode: Calculate power required (kW) for a given flow OR the temperature change (ΔT) a heater or cooler produces.

5. Start Temperature: Density is taken at the start temperature (volumes and flow rates are measured there, so this sets the mass). Specific heat (cp) is taken at the mean of the start and end temperatures.

6. Velocity Check: In Flow mode, enter a pipe internal diameter or duct size to see the velocity, plus the smallest size that keeps it within typical limits.

7. Share & Save: Your last inputs are remembered. Share result sends a summary with a link that reopens the same calculation. Saved Calculations keeps a named list of jobs on this device.

8. Display: Switch Day or Night mode from the top of the app.

9. Install: Add the app to your home screen to open it full screen and use it offline.

Install Time to Heat

Add it to your home screen for full-screen, offline use.

  1. Open this page in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button ⬆︎ (square with an arrow) in the toolbar. On newer iOS, tap ••• first, then Share.
  3. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen.
  4. Make sure Open as Web App is on (if shown), then tap Add.

Chrome and Edge on iPhone also work: tap Share (in the address bar or ••• menu), then Add to Home Screen.
